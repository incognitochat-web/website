## Where the money goes

Donations go to [our Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/{{< param "openCollective" >}}), held by Open Source Collective, a non-profit that holds money for open-source projects. (Open Collective is the website; Open Source Collective holds the money.) Every donation and every expense is listed there publicly, so anyone can see what comes in and what it pays for.

Open Source Collective keeps 10% of each donation for its work, and the payment processor (such as Stripe or PayPal) takes its own fee. Open Collective also asks for a tip for itself, already filled in; you can change it, down to zero. Donations aren't tax-deductible.

## Your privacy

- Open Collective asks for your email, which we, as the collective's admins, can see, and lists donors publicly under the name they give.
- With an Open Collective account you can give **incognito**: only the amount is public. We, Open Source Collective, Open Collective and the payment processor may still see who you are.
- Donating has nothing to do with the app: there's no donate button in it, nothing links a donation to your account, and donors get nothing extra.
- This page shows no names: only amounts and dates, fetched from Open Collective when the site is published, without asking for anything else.
